BURNOUT

Corporate Survival Card Game

Everyone works. Not everyone survives.

How to Play

  • Everyone starts with 100 HP
  • Each turn: lose 5 HP (burnout), draw 2 cards, play up to 3
  • Use recovery cards to heal, action cards to attack others
  • Last employee standing wins!
