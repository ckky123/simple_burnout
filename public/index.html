BURNOUT

Corporate Survival Card Game

Everyone works. Not everyone survives.

How to Play

  • Everyone starts with 100 HP
  • Each turn: lose 10 HP (burnout drain), draw 2 cards, forced to play 1 damage card if holding any
  • Then play up to 3 cards from your hand
  • Use recovery cards to heal, action cards to attack others
  • Burnout Drain: You lose 10 HP every turn automatically — this is the stress of corporate life wearing you down. Heal or die!
  • Last employee standing wins!
